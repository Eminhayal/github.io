What does Fişlem. do?

  • Reads receipts and invoices: The store, date, items and total are read on your phone. Add online order invoices as a PDF or screenshot.
  • Keeps your budget: Monthly budget, categories, recurring and daily expenses.
  • Tracks cards separately: Import a statement PDF; a receipt you scanned isn't counted again.
  • Separates your business: Keep documents with tax and photos, then send your accountant a PDF and Excel table at month end.

FAQ

Do I need an account?

No. Fişlem. has no accounts and doesn't connect to your bank.

Where is my data?

Your expenses, receipts and cards are stored only on your phone with iOS data protection. Fişlem. doesn't send them to a server. Files are sent only when you share them.

Is receipt scanning free?

Yes. You get 15 scans each month; when they run out, watch an ad to get 5 more. Adding manually is always free. The main tabs show ads. Your spending, receipt or card data is never given to the ad system.

Will it ask for my card number?

No. It never asks for your full card number, CVV, national ID or bank password. Cards are identified by their last 4 digits only.

How do I send documents to my accountant?

In the Business space, tap Send to accountant. A PDF with photos and a CSV are prepared on your phone, and you send them by email or the share sheet.

What does it run on?

iPhones with iOS 17 or later. Turkish and English.
