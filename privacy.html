Fişlem. Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Fişlem. (“the app”) is developed by Emin Hayal.

We collect no data ourselves. The app has no accounts and never sends your expenses, receipts, cards or statements to the developer or any third party on its own. It uses no analytics tools.

Ads. The app’s main tabs show Google AdMob banner ads, and some actions you choose offer rewarded ads. Ads are not personalized and your spending, receipt or card data is never given to the ad system. Google may collect device, usage and diagnostic data to serve ads (see https://policies.google.com/privacy). In regions such as Europe, you can change your choices in Settings › Ad preferences.

Stored on your device. Your expenses, budget, cards (name and last 4 digits only), statement summaries and, if you choose, receipt photos are stored only on your phone, encrypted with iOS data protection. Deleting the app deletes them. You can also erase everything in Settings › Delete all data.

Camera. The camera is used only while the receipt scanner is open. Text is read on the phone using Apple's on-device text recognition. On supported devices, Apple's on-device language model may help with receipts that can't otherwise be read; it also runs on the phone.

Statements. Statement PDFs you import are read on the phone. If a PDF is password-protected, the password is used only to open it and is not stored.

Business documents and the accountant package. Documents in the Business space (including tax, document number, seller tax ID and photos) are stored on your phone. When you choose Send to accountant, a PDF and CSV are prepared on the phone. You send them through Mail or the share sheet and choose the recipient; from then on, the terms of the app and recipient you chose apply. Your accountant's email stays only on your phone. Fişlem. never sends these files on its own.

Digital invoices. Invoice PDFs or screenshots you add are read on the phone and sent nowhere. We never connect to your email or store accounts. Buyer name, address and ID numbers are not saved to the record; if you keep receipt images, an image of the first page stays only on your phone.

Never requested. We never ask for your full card number, CVV, national ID number, or bank username or password.

Backup and export. Backup and CSV files are created only by you, and you choose where to save them. These files are not encrypted; keep them somewhere you trust.

Notifications. Reminders are scheduled on the phone. By default, amounts and bank names are hidden on the lock screen.

Children. The app is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.
